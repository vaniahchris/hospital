# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Hospital administrators and care staff at Value Family Hospital (Kitende). They use the tool during the workday on a laptop or desktop, often between clinical or ops tasks. The job to be done is: collect patient feedback, understand what went wrong or well, and reach patients by SMS when needed.

## Product Purpose

A feedback administration system for Value Family Hospital. Patients submit outpatient and inpatient feedback; staff manage questions, review responses, maintain a patient contact list, and send SMS (broadcast, single, feedback invite) via Africa's Talking. Success looks like clear response review, reliable patient messaging, and less friction than spreadsheets or generic survey tools.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clinical, trustworthy. Quiet confidence over marketing energy. The interface should feel like a reliable hospital tool, not a startup landing page.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI SaaS (purple gradients, glow, pill spam, hero-metric dashboards)
- Dark neon "ops console" themes
- Decorative card grids with icon + heading + fluff copy
- Marketing-heavy admin login panels that overpower the sign-in task

## Design Principles

1. **White canvas first.** The working surface stays white; brand color appears in actions, selection, and light structural accents only.
2. **Task over decoration.** Every screen has one primary job; enlarge that job and demote secondary chrome.
3. **Group by proximity.** Related controls sit close; separate sections with whitespace, not nested chrome.
4. **Familiar over clever.** Standard admin patterns (sidebar, tables, filters) earn trust; invent nothing for flavor.
5. **Logo colors, restrained.** Indigo and teal from the hospital mark; never gradients as atmosphere.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast on text and controls. Support keyboard focus states. Prefer reduced motion defaults for non-essential animation. Forms and tables must remain usable at 320px width and desktop density.
